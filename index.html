Frontend Developer

Job Description

The Junior Web Developer will assist in designing, developing, and maintaining websites and web applications. The successful candidate will work as part of a collaborative team and contribute to a range of client projects.

Key Responsibilities

Develop and maintain websites using modern web technologies. Write clean, efficient, and well-documented code. Test websites to ensure functionality across different devices and browsers. Work closely with designers and senior developers. Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues. Participate in project meetings and team discussions.

Essential Requirements

Relevant qualification in Web Development, Computing, or Information Technology. Knowledge of HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. Understanding of responsive web design principles. Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. Good communication and teamwork abilities. Passion for web development and continuous learning.
